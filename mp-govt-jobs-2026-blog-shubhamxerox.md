## 4. Exam Pattern & Selection Process

### 4.1 MP Police Constable (GD) 2026
- **Stage 1 — Written Exam (CBT):** 100 marks, objective/multiple-choice, computer-based test conducted in multiple shifts across MP. Covers General Knowledge & Current Affairs, General Hindi, General English, General Science, General Mathematics, Reasoning & Mental Ability.
- **Stage 2 — Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Measurement Test (PMT):** 100 marks — running, height/chest measurement as per category and gender.
- **Stage 3 — Document Verification & Medical Examination.**
- Final merit is typically a combination of written exam + PET/PMT scores (confirm exact weightage split on the official rulebook before printing it as fact).

### 4.2 Nayab Tehsildar Departmental Exam 2026
- Two objective papers of 100 marks each (200 total), 2 hours per paper, bilingual (Hindi/English).
- Open only to eligible serving departmental employees — not a fresh-candidate exam.

### 4.3 MPPSC State Service (typical 3-stage pattern, useful for the next cycle too)
- **Prelims:** Two papers — General Studies (100 questions) and CSAT/aptitude paper (qualifying). 2026 cycle introduced **negative marking in Paper I** (1 mark deducted per wrong answer) — a notable change from earlier years.
- **Mains:** Fully General-Studies based from 2026 onward — the optional-subject paper has been removed, making preparation broader but more predictable.
- **Interview/Personality Test:** 175 marks, forms part of final merit along with Mains.

### 4.4 General Negative Marking Note
Negative marking rules differ post-to-post (some MPESB Group exams have no negative marking, MPPSC Prelims Paper I now does). **Do not print a blanket negative-marking claim across every exam** — link each post to its own official rulebook instead, or you risk misleading a reader into skipping/attempting questions incorrectly.

## 6. Career Growth & Long-Term Advantages

- **Promotion route (Police):** Constable → Head Constable → ASI → Sub-Inspector → Inspector, typically via a mix of seniority and departmental competitive exams (like the Subedar/SI exam MPESB conducts periodically).
- **Promotion route (Revenue):** Patwari → Revenue Inspector → Nayab Tehsildar (via departmental limited exam, as currently open) → Tehsildar → Deputy Collector (via further promotion/State Service route).
- **Departmental exams** are a genuine fast-track — the current Nayab Tehsildar 2026 notification is a live example of this pathway for existing Patwari/clerical staff.
- **Pension & post-retirement:** Government employees on NPS receive a lump-sum + annuity-based pension on retirement, along with gratuity and leave encashment as per state rules.
- **Work-life balance & stability** are consistently cited as key draws versus private-sector roles, alongside guaranteed increments and DA revisions.
